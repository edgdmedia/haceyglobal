## The Four Pillars of Our Work

We focus on four interconnected areas that address the root causes of development challenges across Sub-Saharan Africa. Each area is supported by dedicated teams, evidence-based strategies, and a commitment to sustainable outcomes.

---

### Health and Wellbeing

![Health and Wellbeing](images/health-wellbeing.png)

Ensuring that individuals and communities have access to basic physical, mental, and social well-being services.

**Key Outcomes:**
- Trained 150+ community health workers in Nigeria
- Improved healthcare access in rural communities
- Increased vaccination rates and maternal health visits
- Distributed 20,000 insecticide-treated mosquito nets

**Our Role:**
- Designed community health training programs
- Developed educational materials for health workers
- Supported malaria prevention and maternal health initiatives
- Built partnerships with local health organizations

---

### Economic Inclusion

![Economic Inclusion](images/economic-inclusion.png)

Designing and supporting systems that give low-income and marginalized people opportunities to participate in and benefit from economic activities.

**Key Outcomes:**
- Supported 200+ small business owners in accessing financial services
- Helped businesses grow through training in marketing, accounting, and management
- Connected entrepreneurs with microloans and investment opportunities
- Created a network of business mentors and support services

**Our Role:**
- Developed business training programs
- Facilitated access to financial services
- Built a support network for entrepreneurs
- Provided technical assistance for business development

---

### Rights and Advocacy

![Rights and Advocacy](images/rights-advocacy.png)

Strengthening individuals, communities, organizations, and movements to demand and access their rights.

**Key Outcomes:**
- Trained 50+ local journalists and media professionals
- Produced 100+ stories highlighting community issues and development challenges
- Increased media coverage of underreported topics in rural areas
- Strengthened the capacity of local media to drive public awareness and accountability

**Our Role:**
- Developed media training curriculum
- Organized workshops for journalists
- Supported investigative reporting on development issues
- Built partnerships with media organizations

---

### Environmental Sustainability

![Environmental Sustainability](images/environmental-sustainability.png)

Protecting natural resources and ecosystems to ensure they can support current and future generations.

**Key Outcomes:**
- Averted 10,000 tonnes of CO₂ emissions across 6 states in Nigeria
- Improved air quality in high-density communities through clean energy transitions
- Built local capacity for long-term environmental sustainability

**Our Role:**
- Designed environmental sustainability programs
- Conducted research on air quality and emissions
- Supported community-based clean energy initiatives
- Developed strategies for environmental resilience

---

## Our Integrated Approach

Our four practice areas are interconnected. Health outcomes improve when communities have economic opportunities. Rights are strengthened when people have access to information and resources. Environmental sustainability supports long-term health and economic development. By addressing these areas together, we create solutions that are more effective and sustainable.
