# Positioning and Information Architecture

## Market position

HACEY Global is a strategy, innovation, technology and programme delivery organisation for leaders navigating complex operational and digital change.

We begin by understanding the organisation - its people, processes, systems, objectives and constraints. We diagnose the underlying challenge, design and implement the right response, and build the internal capability needed to sustain and improve it.

## Positioning statement

For executives and programme leaders in healthcare, financial services, government, technology, agriculture, education and social impact who need complex transformation to produce practical results, HACEY Global connects strategy, technology, programme execution, organisational adoption and capability transfer. Unlike advisory-only consultancies or platform-first vendors, the work begins with the operating challenge and continues through implementation, enablement and measurable improvement.

## Brand promise

Make complex transformation work in practice.

## Differentiators

1. Diagnosis before prescription.
2. Strategy and implementation in one accountable path.
3. Human-centred transformation grounded in real workflows.
4. Programme discipline across decisions, dependencies and outcomes.
5. Capability transfer designed into delivery.
6. Evidence boundaries that distinguish verified work, capabilities and illustrative concepts.

## Primary audiences and needs

- CEOs and operating leaders: clarity on value, sequence and accountability.
- CIOs, CTOs and CDOs: architecture, delivery, risk and sustainable ownership.
- Transformation and programme leaders: integrated plans, controls, recovery and benefits.
- Healthcare executives: workflow, interoperability, safety, data and adoption.
- Government leaders: accessible services, policy alignment, case work and programme governance.
- Financial-services executives: trust, control, resilience, customer experience and AI adoption.
- Product leaders: validated direction, operating model, delivery and continuous improvement.
- Foundations and social-impact organisations: programme infrastructure, data and sustainable capability.

## Message hierarchy

1. HACEY Global makes complex transformation work in practice.
2. The organisation joins strategy, technology, delivery, adoption and capability transfer.
3. It begins with the organisational problem rather than a predetermined platform.
4. It serves high-consequence and mission-led environments.
5. The HACEY Path takes work from understanding through optimisation.
6. The next action is a discovery conversation about the challenge.

## Homepage headline options considered

1. Make complex transformation work in practice. **Selected.** Specific, active and differentiating.
2. From transformation strategy to sustained capability. Strong but less immediate.
3. Strategy, technology and delivery that your organisation can own. Strong capability-transfer cue but longer.
4. Diagnose the challenge. Deliver the change. Too slogan-like for the primary line.
5. Practical technology for complex organisations. Too narrow; understates strategy and programme work.

## Conversion model

Primary CTA: Start a discovery conversation.

Alternatives:

- Discuss Your Challenge
- Explore a Solution
- Start with a Focused Diagnostic
- Talk to a Transformation Expert

The contact form asks for role-relevant context and offers email as a direct fallback. It avoids a meeting-calendar promise or response-time claim that has not been operationally confirmed.

## Proof model

- **Verified case study:** evidence, client approval and result attribution confirmed.
- **Capability story:** describes relevant experience without over-claiming a technology implementation.
- **Illustrative engagement:** clearly states how HACEY Global would approach a challenge.
- **Representative product concept:** demonstrates product capability without implying deployment or adoption.
