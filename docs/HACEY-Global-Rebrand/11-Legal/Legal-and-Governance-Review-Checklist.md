# Legal and Governance Review Checklist

Website drafts are implemented at `/privacy`, `/cookies`, `/terms`, `/accessibility`, `/responsible-ai` and `/security`.

## Required before public launch

- Confirm the registered legal entity and trading name.
- Confirm governing law, jurisdiction and contracting address.
- Review the Privacy Policy against the actual form, analytics, hosting and email providers.
- Complete a data-flow and international-transfer review for US, Canadian, UK and EEA visitors.
- Create a cookie inventory after the analytics and consent tools are selected.
- Implement consent before any non-essential cookie or storage.
- Review Terms of Use, warranty, liability and intellectual-property language.
- Confirm enquiry retention period and access controls.
- Publish the security-reporting channel and response process.
- Validate accessibility with manual keyboard, screen-reader and zoom testing.
- Define an owner and review date for every policy.

## Required before client or partner claims

- Relationship and scope verified in writing.
- Logo and trademark use approved.
- Result evidence and attribution reviewed.
- Confidentiality and privacy checked.
- Technology partnership or certification status verified with current source documentation.

## Required before product launch

- Product status and commercial terms approved.
- Data-processing terms and subprocessors documented.
- Security architecture and incident process reviewed.
- Accessibility standard and testing completed.
- Support, maintenance, release and end-of-life processes defined.
- Any healthcare, government or financial-services regulatory statements reviewed by qualified specialists.

These drafts are not legal advice and must receive qualified legal review.
