# Proof Register and Case-Study Template

## Proof register

### Existing-site material to validate

| Theme | Existing statement | Evidence needed | Safe interim use |
|---|---|---|---|
| History | HACEY has operated since 2007 | Registration and company history | “Established in 2007” supplied by project owner |
| Programme delivery | More than 40 social-impact programmes supported with talent | Contracts, programme list, role and dates | Capability theme only |
| Expert network | 200+ verified experts | Current roster and definition of verified | Do not publish until confirmed |
| Malaria prevention | Doses and test kits distributed in Oyo State | Procurement, delivery and programme records | Existing-site claim requiring review |
| Advocacy training | Participant counts across states and groups | Training records, dates and programme owner | Existing-site claim requiring review |
| Client or funder logos | Logos visible on current homepage | Relationship, scope, dates and permission | Do not reuse until approved |
| Testimonials | Anonymous quotations on current homepage | Original source and permission | Remove |

### Evidence standard

For every formal case study, collect:

- Executed contract or documented mandate
- Approved client or anonymous descriptor
- Dates and geography
- HACEY Global’s exact role
- Team and delivery partners
- Baseline and result evidence
- Metric definition and attribution boundaries
- Client approval for public wording, logo and quotation
- Security, privacy and confidentiality review
- Named internal content owner and review date

## Case-study template

### Title

Describe the client outcome or challenge in clear language.

### Approved descriptor

Client name, approved anonymous description or “Illustrative engagement.”

### Challenge

What needed to change? What risk or opportunity made the work important?

### Context

Industry, geography, operating environment, users, constraints and starting point.

### HACEY Global role

State what HACEY Global was accountable for and what remained with the client or other partners.

### Approach

How the team understood and diagnosed the problem. Identify evidence and important decisions.

### Solution

The service, process, product, platform, data or operating-model changes designed.

### Implementation

Workstreams, releases, integrations, migration, governance, testing and operational readiness.

### Capability transfer

Training, documentation, tools, coaching, roles, governance and client ownership established.

### Results

Use only verified outcomes. Define timeframe, baseline, unit, source and HACEY Global’s contribution. Avoid attributing whole-programme results to one supplier.

### Lessons

What should another leader take from the engagement?

## Illustrative engagement format

Use the same headings, but replace “Results” with “Measures we would establish.” Add a visible label at the top: **How We Would Approach This Challenge - Illustrative, Not Client Work.**
