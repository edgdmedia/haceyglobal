## 250-word profile

HACEY Global is a strategy, innovation, technology and programme delivery organisation established in 2007 and based in Austin, Texas, United States. We work with organisations navigating complex digital, operational and organisational change, with a primary market focus on North America, the United Kingdom and Europe.

Our work begins with the organisation rather than a predetermined technology. We examine the objectives, people, processes, systems, evidence and constraints shaping the challenge. We then work with leadership, technical teams and frontline staff to diagnose the underlying problem, design the right response and move it through implementation.

Capabilities include digital and technology strategy, artificial intelligence and data, cloud and infrastructure, enterprise platforms, custom software and product engineering, programme and project management, process and operational transformation, and organisational change and capability development. Sector focus includes healthcare and life sciences, financial services, government and public sector, technology, agriculture and AgTech, education and social impact.

The HACEY Path connects seven stages: Understand, Diagnose, Design, Build, Deploy, Enable and Optimise. Each stage is intended to produce a clear decision, working output or measurable improvement. Capability transfer is built into the method so client teams develop the knowledge, tools, governance and confidence needed to operate and improve the solution after implementation.

HACEY Global combines advisory perspective with practical delivery. The aim is not simply to recommend change or install technology, but to help the organisation make the change work in practice and sustain its value.
