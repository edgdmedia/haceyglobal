# Product and Accelerator Concepts

Status: representative concepts. These pages demonstrate product-development capability. They do not imply existing commercial deployment, clients, revenue or market adoption.

## HACEY Pulse

### Target customer

Transformation offices, programme leaders, government portfolios, foundations and multi-partner delivery organisations.

### Problem addressed

Programme leaders lack one trusted view of milestones, risks, decisions, benefits and partner commitments. Information is often dispersed across plans, slide decks, email and reporting tools.

### Core functionality

A configurable programme and portfolio intelligence workspace connecting delivery control with executive decision support.

### Key features

- Portfolio, programme and workstream dashboards
- Milestone and dependency tracking
- Risk, issue, assumption and decision logs
- Benefits and outcome registers
- Partner commitment and action tracking
- Executive reporting packs and exports
- Role-based alerts and review cadence

### Benefits

- A connected programme view
- Faster escalation and decision-making
- Clearer accountability
- Better outcome and benefit traceability
- Reduced manual reporting effort

### Integrations

Microsoft 365, Power BI, CRM, project-management tools, document repositories and enterprise APIs.

### Deployment

Cloud-hosted or client-managed, configured around the organisation’s delivery model and data-residency requirements.

### Security considerations

Role-based access, segregated portfolios, audit trails, encryption, data classification, retention controls and controlled exports.

### Call to action

Explore a programme-intelligence prototype.

## HACEY Nexus

### Target customer

Enterprises, health systems, public agencies and mission-led organisations with dispersed governed knowledge.

### Problem addressed

Important policies, procedures, decisions and expertise are dispersed across documents, repositories and individual memory. Teams waste time searching and may act on outdated information.

### Core functionality

A permission-aware AI knowledge assistant that retrieves, explains and cites approved organisational sources.

### Key features

- Enterprise search and conversational retrieval
- Source citations and document freshness indicators
- Permission-aware answers
- Curated knowledge collections
- User feedback and answer evaluation
- Usage, unanswered-question and quality analytics
- Administrative controls for approved sources

### Benefits

- Faster access to trusted information
- Reduced duplicate questions and research
- Better policy and procedure consistency
- Visible knowledge gaps
- Auditable source use

### Integrations

SharePoint, Microsoft 365, Google Drive, service platforms, learning systems and approved data repositories.

### Deployment

Private cloud or client-controlled deployment with model, region and repository choices shaped around risk and existing architecture.

### Security considerations

Permission synchronisation, prompt and response logging, sensitive-data controls, model evaluation, retention, abuse monitoring and source-level access enforcement.

### Call to action

Assess a governed knowledge-assistant use case.

## HACEY Care

### Target customer

Health systems, care programmes, public-health organisations and digital health service teams.

### Problem addressed

Patients and staff navigate disconnected appointment, communication, education and follow-up processes. Existing systems may contain the data but not provide a coherent engagement journey.

### Core functionality

A configurable patient-engagement layer supporting access, navigation, programme communication and follow-up.

### Key features

- Patient portal modules
- Appointment, referral and follow-up workflows
- Role-appropriate education and care content
- Secure messaging and notifications
- Consent and preference management
- Care-programme task lists
- Engagement and service analytics

### Benefits

- Clearer patient journeys
- More consistent communication
- Improved programme visibility
- Reduced avoidable manual coordination
- A modular path to experience improvement

### Integrations

EHR/EMR, HL7/FHIR APIs, CRM, identity, scheduling, messaging and analytics services.

### Deployment

Modular web and mobile deployment designed around the care setting, patient population, integration environment and compliance requirements.

### Security considerations

Privacy-by-design, consent, identity proofing, authentication, auditability, minimum-necessary access, encryption, accessibility and clinical safety review.

### Call to action

Map a priority patient journey.

## HACEY CaseFlow

### Target customer

Government agencies, social-service providers, compliance teams and grant or programme operators.

### Problem addressed

Case work is slowed by fragmented intake, manual routing, repeated data entry, limited status visibility and inconsistent documentation.

### Core functionality

A configurable case and compliance workflow foundation for intake, eligibility, work management, decisions and reporting.

### Key features

- Accessible digital and staff-assisted intake
- Eligibility and validation rules
- Case assignment and workflow routing
- Task, note and document management
- Reviews, approvals and escalations
- Correspondence and notifications
- Service, compliance and outcome reporting

### Benefits

- Structured and visible case progress
- More consistent policy execution
- Reduced manual coordination
- Auditable decisions
- Better operational insight

### Integrations

Identity, CRM, document management, payment, notification, geospatial and government data interfaces as required.

### Deployment

Configurable cloud deployment with phased process migration, data conversion and role-based rollout.

### Security considerations

Segregation of duties, audit history, data classification, record retention, privacy, accessibility, incident response and controls for high-risk decisions.

### Call to action

Review a case workflow for modernisation.

## Product governance

Before any concept is presented as a commercial product, define the owner, roadmap, support model, service levels, pricing, data-processing terms, security controls, accessibility standard, deployment options, product documentation and release process. Until then, use “representative product concept” or “representative accelerator concept” in every public description.
