## Company overview

HACEY Global is a strategy, innovation, technology and programme delivery organisation. We help leaders understand complex organisational challenges, design the right response and carry it through implementation, adoption and measurable improvement.

Our work begins with the organisation - its people, processes, systems, objectives and constraints - rather than a predetermined platform. We combine advisory perspective with practical delivery and build the internal capability required to operate and improve the solution after implementation.

## Vision and mission

**Vision**

Organisations equipped to turn better decisions and technology into lasting public, human and business value.

**Mission**

We diagnose complex organisational challenges, design and implement practical solutions, and build the internal capability required to operate and improve them.

## Our evolution

Established in 2007, HACEY Global developed through work in programmes, research, health, stakeholder engagement, systems strengthening and social impact. Those disciplines require evidence, practical implementation and the ability to work across policy, people, data and delivery.

The organisation’s current position extends that foundation into enterprise strategy, innovation, technology services, programme management and digital products for North American and European markets.

## The HACEY Path

1. **Understand** - align on objectives, stakeholders, context, evidence and constraints.
2. **Diagnose** - identify root causes, dependencies, risks and critical decisions.
3. **Design** - co-design the target service, operating model, technology, roadmap and measures.
4. **Build** - configure, engineer and integrate in useful increments.
5. **Deploy** - release safely, migrate deliberately and manage readiness.
6. **Enable** - transfer knowledge, tools, roles and decision rights.
7. **Optimise** - measure performance, learn from use and improve continuously.

## Capability transfer

A solution is not fully implemented when the system goes live. It is implemented when people can use it, leaders can govern it and internal teams can improve it.

Capability transfer may include paired delivery, role design, training, playbooks, governance routines, product-management practices, technical documentation, operational handover and coaching.

## Responsible technology principles
