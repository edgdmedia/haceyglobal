## Article standard

Every article should:

1. Answer a real executive or practitioner decision.
2. State the operating problem before the technology.
3. Distinguish evidence, inference and HACEY Global’s recommendation.
4. Link primary or authoritative sources for factual and standards-based claims.
5. Avoid unverified statistics and decorative trend language.
6. Include practical questions or actions.
7. Connect naturally to one capability, solution or industry page.
8. Receive subject-matter, brand and source review before publication.

## Metadata template

- Title: 50-65 characters where practical
- Description: 140-160 characters describing the decision value
- Category
- Author
- Published date and reviewed date
- Reading time
- Canonical URL
- Source links
- Related capability, solution and industry
- Social title and description

## Publication cadence

Begin with two high-quality articles per month. Build one coherent cluster at a time rather than publishing across every category. The first cluster should be Enterprise AI Adoption, followed by Healthcare Interoperability and Technology Programme Delivery.
