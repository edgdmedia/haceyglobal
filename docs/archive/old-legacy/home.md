## Our Impact

### Zero Carbon

![Zero Carbon Impact](images/impact-women-youth.jpg)

**Key Outcomes:**
- Averted 10,000 tonnes of CO₂ emissions across 6 states in Nigeria
- Improved air quality in high-density communities through clean energy transitions
- Built local capacity for long-term environmental sustainability

**Approach:** We helped communities in six Nigerian states transition to cleaner energy sources. By providing training and resources, we enabled households and local businesses to adopt practices that reduce carbon emissions while maintaining productivity.

**Our Role:** We developed and implemented strategies to reduce harmful emissions and improve air quality in communities facing significant environmental challenges.

---

### Women and Youth Empowerment

![Women and Youth Empowerment](images/impact-women-youth.jpg)

**Key Outcomes:**
- 10,000 women and youth engaged in economic empowerment activities
- Trained over 1,000 women to develop financial literacy skills
- Supported 500 women in launching small businesses
- 500+ youth connected to vocational training and job opportunities

**Approach:** We trained women and young people in financial literacy and business development. This included helping them create business plans, connect with funding opportunities, and build sustainable livelihoods.

**Our Role:** We designed programs to help women and young people develop the skills and confidence needed to start businesses and manage their finances effectively.

---

### Health Worker Training

![Health Worker Training](images/impact-health-workers.jpg)

**Key Outcomes:**
- Trained 150+ community health workers in Nigeria
- Improved healthcare access in rural communities
- Increased vaccination rates and maternal health visits
- Built a scalable model for community health training

**Approach:** We provided training to community health workers on basic healthcare practices, including maternal health, child nutrition, and disease prevention. This helped improve healthcare access in rural areas where formal medical facilities are limited.

**Our Role:** We developed training materials, organized workshops, and mentored health workers to ensure they could deliver effective care at the community level.

---

### Malaria Prevention

![Malaria Prevention](images/impact-health.jpg)

**Key Outcomes:**
- Distributed 20,000 insecticide-treated mosquito nets
- Reduced malaria incidence by 30% in target communities
- Trained local health workers to conduct malaria screening and prevention education
- Built a replicable model for malaria prevention in rural areas

**Approach:** We partnered with local health organizations to distribute treated nets and educate communities on malaria prevention. This included training health workers to identify early symptoms and refer patients for treatment.

**Our Role:** We organized distribution drives, conducted community education sessions, and worked with health workers to ensure long-term adoption of prevention practices.

---

### Business Support

![Business Support](images/impact-business.jpg)

**Key Outcomes:**
- Supported 200+ small business owners in accessing financial services
- Helped businesses grow through training in marketing, accounting, and management
- Connected entrepreneurs with microloans and investment opportunities
- Created a network of business mentors and support services

**Approach:** We provided training and resources to small business owners, helping them improve their operations, access funding, and expand their customer base. This included workshops on financial management, marketing strategies, and business planning.

**Our Role:** We developed business training programs, facilitated access to financial services, and built a support network for entrepreneurs to share knowledge and resources.

---

### Media for Empowerment

![Media for Empowerment](images/impact-media.jpg)

**Key Outcomes:**
- Trained 50+ local journalists and media professionals
- Produced 100+ stories highlighting community issues and development challenges
- Increased media coverage of underreported topics in rural areas
- Strengthened the capacity of local media to drive public awareness and accountability

**Approach:** We trained journalists to cover important community stories, including health, education, and economic issues. This helped raise awareness about challenges faced by underserved populations and held decision-makers accountable.

**Our Role:** We developed a media training curriculum, organized workshops, and supported journalists in producing quality reporting on development-related topics.
