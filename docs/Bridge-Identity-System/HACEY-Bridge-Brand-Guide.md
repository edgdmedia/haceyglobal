# HACEY Global - Strategy-to-Execution Bridge Brand Guide

**Identity direction:** Selected concept 02  
**Status:** Approved creative direction; production vector reconstruction and trademark clearance required before registration or high-volume use  
**Version:** 1.0 - 21 September 2026

## 1. The brand story

Transformation rarely fails because organisations lack ambition. It fails in the space between a sound decision and a change that works in practice.

HACEY Global exists to close that space.

We connect strategy to execution, technology to operating reality, and external expertise to internal capability. We begin with the real challenge, make the critical decisions visible, and stay with the work through design, delivery, adoption and improvement. The result is not simply a recommendation or a deployed platform. It is a stronger organisation that can keep moving after the engagement ends.

The Strategy-to-Execution Bridge expresses this role. Two disciplined pillars face one another. One represents intent: strategy, evidence and the case for change. The other represents realised value: working services, adopted technology and measurable performance. The central lime square is the connection point where decisions become coordinated action. The negative space creates an H without closing the system, signalling structure with openness.

### Short story

HACEY Global bridges the distance between a good decision and durable change. We connect strategy, technology, delivery and adoption so organisations can implement confidently and retain the capability to keep improving.

### Brand promise

**Make complex transformation work in practice.**

### Positioning statement

HACEY Global is a strategy, technology and transformation consultancy for organisations that need practical implementation, accountable delivery and capability that stays with their teams.

## 2. What the mark means

### Left pillar - strategic intent

Represents evidence, ambition, priorities and the decisions that define a credible direction.

### Right pillar - operational reality

Represents systems, people, implementation, adoption and measurable outcomes.

### Central connector - the HACEY intervention

The Signal Lime square is the high-value connection: a decision, product increment, governance mechanism or capability that converts intent into progress.

### Open bridge - capability transfer

The mark is structured without being sealed. The open centre represents collaboration, transparent decision-making and capability that remains inside the client organisation.

## 4. Clear space and minimum size

Let **x** equal the width of the central connector square.

- Keep at least `1x` clear space around every side of a full lockup.
- Use `1.5x` where the logo sits near another organisation's mark or a page edge.
- Horizontal lockup minimum: 180 px digital or 32 mm print.
- Stacked lockup minimum: 150 px digital or 28 mm print.
- Emblem minimum: 24 px digital or 6 mm print.
- Below the minimum size, use the emblem only and remove any surrounding visual detail.

Clear space may contain the background colour but no text, rules, icons, partner marks or image focal points.

- 65-75% Warm Ivory, White or Warm Paper
- 20-30% Ink and Bridge Teal
- 5-10% Signal Lime

Signal Lime is a decision point, not a decorative fill. Use it for one primary action, the connector in the mark, active states and selected data highlights.

### Accessibility

- Use Ink on Warm Ivory, Warm Paper or White for body copy.
- Use White or Warm Ivory on Ink for reversed body copy.
- Use Ink text on Signal Lime; never use white text on Signal Lime.
- Do not use Signal Lime for small text on light backgrounds.
- Do not communicate status through colour alone.
- Validate new digital colour combinations against WCAG 2.2 AA.

## 6. Typography

### Important wordmark rule

The logo wordmark is artwork. Never recreate, typeset, stretch or approximate it with a font. Always use an approved logo asset. The typography below is the supporting brand system, not a replacement for the logo lettering.

### Primary family - Geist

Use **Geist** for headlines, body text, navigation, buttons, tables and interface controls. It supports the identity because it is direct, contemporary and highly readable without competing with the geometric mark.

Recommended weights:

- Geist Regular 400 - body copy, captions and data labels
- Geist Medium 500 - navigation and supporting headings
- Geist SemiBold 600 - buttons, strong labels and H3 headings
- Geist Bold 700 - H2 headings and compact emphasis
- Geist ExtraBold 800 - short display statements only

Fallback stack: `Geist, Inter, Arial, sans-serif`.

### Technical accent - IBM Plex Mono

Use **IBM Plex Mono Medium 500** for stage numbers, categories, metadata, eyebrow labels, document-control information and small technical annotations.

Fallback stack: `IBM Plex Mono, Consolas, monospace`.

Do not use the mono family for paragraphs, large headlines or entire tables.

## 7. Graphic language

The wider identity borrows three ideas from the bridge mark:

1. **Paired structures** - place strategy and implementation, current and target, or risk and response in deliberate visual relationship.
2. **Connection points** - use a single Lime square, node or short rule to indicate the decision that links them.
3. **Open systems** - favour whitespace, visible grids and incomplete frames over closed decorative containers.

Use flat colour, fine structural lines, numbered stages, asymmetrical editorial layouts and clear information hierarchy. Avoid default card grids, glossy gradients, 3D effects, neural brains, robots, circuit-board backgrounds and decorative technology spectacle.

## 8. Brand voice

### Voice attributes

- Intelligent, without performing expertise
- Calm around complexity
- Direct, without becoming abrupt
- Collaborative, with clear accountability
- Practical, without sacrificing ambition
- Responsible, without hiding behind policy language

### Writing principles

1. Start with the decision, problem or outcome.
2. Use specific nouns and active verbs.
3. Explain what the work produces.
4. Connect strategy, people, process, data and technology.
5. Distinguish verified evidence from proposed capability.
6. End with a practical next move.

### Message architecture

- **Master message:** Make complex transformation work in practice.
- **Supporting message:** One accountable path from decision to durable change.
- **Proof idea:** Strategy, technology, delivery and adoption are connected rather than handed off.
- **Capability idea:** The knowledge and capability stay with the client team.
- **Call to action:** Bring us the challenge, not a predetermined solution.

## 9. Incorrect use

Do not:

- Alter the relationship between the emblem and wordmark outside the approved lockups.
- Recreate the wordmark with live type.
- Move, resize or recolour the central connector independently.
- Use unapproved gradients, drop shadows, bevels or outlines.
- Stretch, compress, rotate or skew the logo.
- Place the full-colour mark on a low-contrast or visually busy image.
- Set the mark inside an arbitrary container or badge.
- Add service names, slogans or location text inside the clear-space zone.
- Use Signal Lime as a large background behind the full-colour logo.
- Present the emblem alone on first-contact legal or procurement documents.

## 10. Application guidance

### Website

Use the primary horizontal lockup in the header on Warm Ivory. Use the emblem for the favicon and compact mobile states. Keep Signal Lime focused on the main action.

### Proposals and reports

Use the primary lockup on light covers or the reversed lockup on Ink covers. Use the one-colour version for internal pages, stamps and low-colour printing.

### Presentations

Use the emblem as a quiet footer identifier. Use the full lockup on the opening and closing slides. Build diagrams with paired structures and a single Lime connection point.

### Social profiles

Use the emblem-only asset for avatars. Use the horizontal or stacked lockup in banners. Keep the mark away from platform cropping and interface overlays.

### Co-branding

Use the one-colour or primary horizontal lockup. Match optical height, not absolute width. Separate partner marks with at least `1.5x` clear space and a neutral divider when needed.

## 11. Governance and production

- The selected concept is an approved creative direction, not yet a legally cleared trademark.
- Commission a precise vector reconstruction in SVG, EPS and PDF before large-format, manufacturing or registration use.
- Complete a professional trademark search in the intended jurisdictions and service classes.
- Preserve one master asset source and export approved formats from it.
- The brand owner approves logo exceptions, new colourways and co-branding arrangements.
- The digital owner verifies accessibility, responsive sizing and metadata.
- Review this guide annually or after a material change in positioning.
